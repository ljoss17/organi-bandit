# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes contributions from everyone. To keep it a positive place to participate:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or behavior.
- No sharing someone else's private information without their consent.

Violations may result in a warning, removal of content, or being blocked from the project, at the maintainer's discretion.
